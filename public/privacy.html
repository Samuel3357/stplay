ST PLAY

Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · 15/10/2026

1. Summary

  • ST PLAY has no ads, no tracking and does not sell data.

2. Data that stays on your device

  • History, favorites, playback positions and settings are stored locally on the device and are not sent to us.

3. Your playlist (pairing from your phone)

  • When you use the QR code, the playlist you enter (M3U link, or Xtream server, username and password) is sent to our server, stored ENCRYPTED (AES-256-GCM) and tied only to your device identifier (as a hash). It is kept until you remove it, on the TV (Settings) or on the website (My playlist tab), so that it comes back by itself if you reinstall the app.
  • Only your device can retrieve the playlist. We do not log its contents (links, usernames or passwords) and we never access your playlist address.

4. Free trial

  • To enforce the 10-day trial we store an irreversible hash of a device identifier and the start date. We do not store your name, email or the raw identifier.

5. Security and providers

  • We use HTTPS and per-IP rate limits (temporary counters). The site is hosted on Vercel, which may keep technical access logs (such as IP) under its own policy. Purchases are processed by Roku; we receive no payment data.

6. Your rights

  • You can delete your playlist at any time (TV or website) and request information or deletion of trial data by writing to reizindofogo@gmail.com.

7. Children

  • The app is not directed to children.

8. Contact
