ST PLAY

Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · 15/10/2026

1. Summary

  • ST PLAY has no ads, no tracking and does not sell data.

2. Data that stays on your device

  • Your playlists (including Xtream username and password), history, favorites and settings are stored locally on the device and are not sent to us.

3. Pairing a playlist from your phone

  • When you use the QR code, the playlist passes through our server only to reach the TV: it is encrypted, kept for at most 15 minutes and deleted as soon as the TV receives it. We do not log its contents (links, usernames or passwords).

4. Free trial

  • To enforce the 10-day trial we store an irreversible hash of a device identifier and the start date. We do not store your name, email or the raw identifier.

5. Security and providers

  • We use HTTPS and per-IP rate limits (temporary counters). The site is hosted on Vercel, which may keep technical access logs (such as IP) under its own policy. Purchases are processed by Roku; we receive no payment data.

6. Your rights

  • You may request information or deletion of trial data by writing to [E-MAIL DE CONTATO]. Removing the playlist from the device erases local data.

7. Children

  • The app is not directed to children.
